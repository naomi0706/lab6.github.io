Mongolia's Finest
Luxury Hotel

For over six decades, the Ulaanbaatar Hotel has stood as a symbol of elegance and distinction in the heart of Mongolia's capital. As the country's first internationally recognized five-star property, we have welcomed presidents, artists, explorers, and discerning travelers from every corner of the globe.

Located in the prestigious Sukhbaatar District on B.Altangerel Street, our hotel blends classic luxury with modern sophistication. Each of our meticulously designed rooms and suites offers breathtaking city views, premium bedding, and amenities tailored to every preference.

Beyond accommodation, Ulaanbaatar Hotel is a destination in itself — featuring award-winning dining at Arig Anya Japanese Restaurant and the historic Bar established in 1961, along with a state-of-the-art fitness center, Magnolia Coffee Shop, and versatile event spaces for weddings, corporate meetings, and private celebrations.

Hotel Facilities

Ulaanbaatar Hotel offers an exceptional range of dining and leisure facilities for guests and visitors. From authentic Japanese cuisine at Arig Anya to craft cocktails at our historic bar, every experience is crafted with care. Arig Anya

Step into our Japanese noodle restaurant, where you can savor the authentic flavors of Japan. Enjoy a diverse selection of ramen, udon, and soba dishes, all crafted with fresh ingredients and traditional recipes. A unique dining experience in the heart of Ulaanbaatar.
